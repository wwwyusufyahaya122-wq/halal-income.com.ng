How To Spot Fake Oraimo Power Bank in 30 Seconds

By: Yusuf Yahaya - | Updated: November 24, 2026 | Read time: 8 min | Verify: LinkedIn

I bought a fake "20000mAh" Oraimo power bank in Computer Village for ₦10,000. It charged my phone once and died. The seller said "Original, my brother." It was not original.

I lost ₦10,000 that day. This guide is so you don't lose your own ₦10,000. Do these 5 checks before you pay - in Slot, Computer Village, or when your Jumia order arrives.

Quick Rule: If ANY check fails, don't pay. Walk away. A real power bank passes ALL 5 checks. A fake fails at least 2.

Quick Summary Table - Save This

1. Weight20000mAh = 340-360g190-220g, feels light

5. Swelling / SmellFlat, no smell, no heatSwollen, hot, chemical smell

Check 1: Weight - The Fastest Check (10 Seconds)

Real 20000mAh power bank has real batteries inside. Real batteries are heavy.

  • Real 10000mAh: 190-210g
  • Real 20000mAh: 340-360g
  • Real 27000mAh: 430-470g

How to check: If you have a kitchen scale, put it on it. If you don't, hold it in one hand and your phone in the other. Real 20000mAh feels like 2 phones. Fake feels like 1 phone.

My fake "20000mAh" weighed 202g. I knew immediately - it was fake. Fake sellers put sand or small battery inside to make it look big.

Check 2: Seal, Box and Warranty Card (10 Seconds)

Open the box slowly. Don't let seller rush you.

Real Oraimo box: Printing is clear, colours are sharp, spelling is correct. Seal is tight. Inside you get: power bank, short cable, warranty card, manual.

Fake box: Printing is blurry, colours look washed, "Oraimo" spelling may be "Oraino" or "Oraimo" with wrong font. No warranty card. Cable is very thin and short.

What to do: If there is no warranty card, don't buy. Real Oraimo always has warranty card in Nigeria. No card = no warranty = no return.

Check 3: QR Code - Scratch and Scan (30 Seconds)

This is the most important check. Do it in the shop.

Steps:

1. Find the silver sticker on the box. Scratch it gently with coin.
2. You will see a code + QR code.
3. Open your phone camera, scan QR code.
4. It should open Oraimo official website.

Real: Website says "Original Product" and "Checked 1 time".
Fake: Website opens random site, or says "This code has been checked 487 times". That means 487 people checked same fake code. Don't buy.

Tip: If seller says "Don't scratch, you will spoil it" - walk away. Real seller wants you to scratch and check.

Check 4: Charging Test In The Shop (10 Minutes)

Don't pay before you test. Tell seller: "Let me test for 10 minutes."

How to test:

1. Check your phone is at 20% or less.
2. Plug your original cable into power bank, plug phone.
3. Wait 10 minutes.
4. Check phone percentage.

Real 20000mAh: Phone goes from 20% to 35-40% in 10 minutes. Power bank stays cool.
Fake: Phone goes from 20% to 23-25% in 10 minutes. Power bank gets very hot. Sometimes phone says "Charging slowly".

If seller refuses to let you test, don't buy. Real shop like Slot will let you test.

Check 5: Swelling, Heat and Smell

Put power bank on flat table. Look from side.

Real: Flat, no bulge, no smell, no heat when not charging.
Fake: One side is swollen like pillow, smells like chemical, gets hot even when not charging.

STOP if it is swelling. Don't charge it. Don't keep it on bed. It can explode. Take photo, keep receipt, return it. This happens with fake power banks after 1-2 weeks in Nigerian heat.

Where Is Safest To Buy Original In Nigeria?

I am not selling you anything. But if you want original, buy from places where you can return:

  • Slot - Best for warranty. You can walk in and return in 7 days.
  • Jumia Official Store - Only buy if seller name says "Official Store" and rating is above 4.5. Check return policy is 7 days.
  • Konga - Check seller is official, check warranty card inside.

Avoid: Roadside seller with no shop, no receipt, no return. If it is too cheap - 50000mAh for ₦10,000 - it is fake. Real 20000mAh costs ₦25,000+ in Nigeria in 2026. If price is too good, it is fake.

What To Do If You Already Bought Fake?

1. Stop using it. Don't charge it if it is swelling.
2. Take photo of box, QR code, receipt.
3. Go back to seller within 7 days. Show photo, ask for refund.
4. If seller refuses, keep photo. Don't use fake power bank - it can damage your phone battery and can explode.

Questions Nigerians Ask Me

Q: How much should real Oraimo 20000mAh weigh?
A: 340-360g. Fake weighs 190-220g.
Q: Can I check fake without scratching QR?
A: Yes, use weight + charging test + box check. But QR is best. Always scratch and check before you pay.
Q: Is it better to buy in shop or online?
A: If it's your first time, buy in shop - Slot or 3Star - so you can weigh, check QR, and test. Once you know how to check, you can buy online from Official Store.
