What is Takaful? Watch 20s Explanation

How Islamic Insurance Works in 20 Seconds

Takaful = Mutual Help. No Interest. Profits shared with you.

Takaful is the Halal alternative to conventional insurance. Instead of paying premiums to a company that invests in interest, Muslims contribute to a mutual pool. When a member has a claim, the pool pays. Any surplus is distributed back to participants.
