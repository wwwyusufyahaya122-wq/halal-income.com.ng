❌ 5 Mistakes Nigerians Make When Buying Gadgets (Don't Do This)

Mistake 1: Buying "UK Used" Laptop Without Warranty. 70% die in 3 months. Always ask for warranty card + test battery for 1 hour before paying.
Mistake 2: Buying Power Bank by mAh Only. Fake 50000mAh power banks are actually 10000mAh. Check weight + charging time. Real 20000mAh weighs ~350g.
Mistake 3: Ignoring Charger Voltage. If charger says 110V, it will burn in Nigeria. Must be 100-240V / 220V.
Mistake 4: Trusting Jumia Reviews Only. Many are paid. Check our 14-day test + Slot price before you buy.
Mistake 5: Buying From Instagram Vendors Without Location. If they have no shop in Lagos/Abuja, no return policy, don't send money first. Use Pay on Delivery.
