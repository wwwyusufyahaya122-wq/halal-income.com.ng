How to Increase Your Loan Limit in Nigeria in 2026

  1. Borrow small and pay back early 2-3 times: This builds trust. Start with ₦5,000 and pay in 7 days.
  2. Link your salary account: ₦100k+ monthly salary = higher limit. Carbon and Kuda love salary earners.
  3. Use the app for bills and savings: Active users who pay DSTV, buy airtime get bigger offers.
  4. Keep a good credit score: No defaults. CRC Credit Bureau shares data with all loan apps.
  5. Update your details: Add work ID, utility bill, and BVN. Complete profile = higher limit.

Frequently Asked Questions - FAQ

Q1: Can I get a loan without BVN in Nigeria?
A: No. BVN is mandatory for all CBN licensed loan apps in Nigeria 2026. Avoid any app that says "No BVN".
Q2: What happens if I don't pay back my loan?
A: The app will report you to CRC Credit Bureau, call your contacts, and block you. It will be hard to get loans again.
Q3: Which loan app has the lowest interest rate?
A: Kuda Bank Loan and Carbon at 1.5% per month. But requirements are strict.
Q4: How fast can I get money?
A: Most apps like FairMoney and Palmcredit pay within 5-10 minutes after approval to your bank.
Q5: Are loan apps legal in Nigeria?
A: Yes, if they are licensed by CBN and registered with FCCPC. Check the list above.
