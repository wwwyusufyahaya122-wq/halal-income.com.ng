🔧 Fix It Yourself - No Need To Pay Repair Man

Before you pay ₦15,000 to repair man, try these. These are the 4 fixes Nigerians ask me most:

1. Phone Charging Slow?

Clean your charging port with a toothpick - gently. Dust builds up in 3 months. Then check cable. 70% of slow charging is fake cable, not phone. Use original cable + original charger. If it still charges slow, try another socket - some sockets in Nigeria have low voltage.

2. Earbuds One Side Not Working?

Don't throw them away. Reset: Put both earbuds in case, close lid, wait 10 seconds, open lid, hold button on both for 10 seconds until light flashes. Delete old Bluetooth pairing on your phone, pair again. This fixes 80% of Oraimo, Anker, Samsung earbuds.

3. Laptop Overheating?

Put it on flat table, not bed. Bed blocks fan. Clean fan with blower. Don't use laptop while charging in hot room with no fan. If it still overheats, check Task Manager - close Chrome tabs you are not using. Chrome uses a lot of RAM and heat.

4. Power Bank Swelling?

STOP using it immediately. Don't charge it. Don't keep it on bed. It can explode. Take photo, keep receipt, return to seller within 7 days. This happens with fake power banks after 1-2 weeks in Nigerian heat.

⚡ NEPA Survival Guide - What You Actually Need

NEPA took light for 9 hours in Abuja last week. Here's what I learned, and what you should do:

1. Don't buy 50000mAh for ₦10k. It is fake. Real 20000mAh costs ₦25k+. Fake will swell and waste your money.

2. Buy one good power bank with warranty, not two cheap fakes. One real 20000mAh charges phone 4-5 times. Two fakes charge phone 1 time each and die.

3. Buy original Type-C cable. Fake cable charges slow and damages battery. Original cable costs ₦3,000-₦5,000 but lasts 1 year.

4. Charge power bank at night when light is stable. Don't leave it charging on bed. Charge on table, away from curtain.

5. How to make phone last longer during NEPA: Turn on Battery Saver, turn brightness to 40%, turn off background data for TikTok/Instagram, turn on Dark Mode. This gives you 3-4 extra hours.

🎓 Student Guide - Laptop, Phone & Power Bank For School

If you are going to UNILAG, ABU, UNIJOS, or any university in Nigeria, you need 3 things that actually work:

  • Laptop: 8GB RAM minimum, SSD (not HDD), battery 8 hours, weight under 2kg. Don't buy by "Core i7" name. Buy by battery + weight.
  • Phone: Battery 5000mAh+, 128GB storage, good camera for assignments. Don't buy phone with 64GB - you will run out of space in 1 semester.
  • Power Bank: 20000mAh real, with warranty. You will need it for lectures, hostel, and NEPA.

Mistake to avoid: Don't buy UK Used laptop with no warranty. 70% have bad battery. If you must buy UK Used, test battery for 1 hour in shop, check all keys work, ask for 30-day warranty in writing.

❓ Questions Nigerians Ask Me Every Day

Q: How do I know if Jumia seller is selling original?
A: Check seller name is "Official Store", check ratings over 4.5, check return policy is 7 days. If seller is new with 0 ratings, don't buy.
Q: Is it better to buy from Slot or Jumia?
A: Slot is better for warranty - you can walk in and return. Jumia is better for price - but buy only from Official Store.
Q: Why does my new phone battery drain fast?
A: First 3 days, phone is updating apps in background. Wait 3 days, turn off background data for TikTok/Instagram, and check again.
Q: Should I buy power bank online or in shop?
A: If it's your first time, buy in shop - Slot or 3Star - so you can weigh it, check QR, and test it. Once you know how to check, you can buy online from Official Store.
