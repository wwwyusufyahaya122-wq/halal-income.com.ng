✅ The 5 Checks - Do These Before You Pay For ANY Gadget

Use this checklist in Slot, Computer Village, Banex Plaza, or when your Jumia order arrives. If ANY check fails, don't pay—return it.

Check What To Do Real = Pass Fake = Fail
1. Weight Put it on a scale or feel its density 20000mAh = 340–360g (feels solid) ~200g, feels hollow or unusually light
2. Seal + Box Check factory seal, printing, and warranty card Sharp print, intact seal, warranty card inside Blurry text, misspelled words, no warranty card
3. QR Code Scratch silver sticker and scan with phone Opens official brand site and confirms 1st verification Opens random URL or says "checked 500+ times"
4. Charger Read small input text printed on charger Says 100–240V / 220V (safe for Nigerian power) Says 110V only—will burn out quickly
5. Test In Shop Plug phone at 20% for 10 minutes before leaving Charges steadily without overheating Battery % drops, disconnects, or gets hot fast
Safety Warning: Never buy a power bank or phone battery that looks slightly swollen or smells like sweet chemicals. Lithium batteries that swell are a fire hazard.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if a power bank is fake in Nigeria?

A genuine 20,000mAh power bank weighs around 340g to 360g, charges a standard 5,000mAh phone 3 to 4 full times, and includes a valid scratch-off QR verification sticker and warranty card. Fakes usually weigh around 200g (often filled with sand or cheap cells) and die after 1 charge.
