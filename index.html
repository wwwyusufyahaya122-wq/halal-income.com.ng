What is Takaful? Halal Insurance Explained

Takaful is Islamic insurance based on mutual cooperation. Members contribute to a pool and help each other. No interest, no gambling. Approved by Shariah scholars in Nigeria.

  1. No Riba: No interest involved
  2. No Gharar: No excessive uncertainty
  3. Profit Sharing: Surplus is shared back to members

How to Choose Insurance in Nigeria

  1. Check NAICOM License: Only buy from NAICOM approved companies
  2. Ask about Takaful: If you need Halal option, choose Cornerstone or Helios
  3. Compare 3 quotes: Don't buy the first one you see
  4. Check claims process: Can you claim via WhatsApp/App?
