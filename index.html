Pros and Cons of Top 5 Loan Apps

1. FairMoney

Pros: Very high loan limit, fast approval in 5 minutes, CBN licensed
Cons: High interest if you pay late, requires good bank history

2. Carbon

Pros: Lowest interest rates, offers investment + savings, trusted brand
Cons: Strict requirements, slower approval for new users

3. Palmcredit

Pros: No collateral, instant disbursement, easy for first timers
Cons: Very high daily interest, short repayment time

4. Opay Loan

Pros: Available to all Opay users, flexible repayment, low minimum
Cons: Small loan amounts at start, must use Opay wallet

5. Kuda Bank Loan

Pros: Lowest interest rate, up to ₦10M, 12 months to repay
Cons: Must have Kuda account for 3+ months, salary account needed

How to Increase Your Loan Limit in Nigeria

  1. Borrow small and pay back early 2-3 times: This builds trust.
  2. Link your salary account: ₦100k+ salary = higher limit.
  3. Use the app for bills and savings: Active users get bigger offers.
  4. Keep a good credit score: No defaults.
  5. Update your details: Add work ID, utility bill, and BVN.
