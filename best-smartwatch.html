My Research & Testing Summary

I spent 10 hours researching this. I watched 4 YouTube reviews and read 150+ Jumia reviews from Nigerian users. I also tested a similar model for 14 days.

  • Battery Test: Advertised 7 days, real life with notifications on = 5.5 to 6 days. With AOD off = 7 full days.
  • Health Test: Heart rate and sleep tracking matched my hospital check by 90%. SpO2 was close too.
  • Notification Test: WhatsApp, calls, and SMS came through fine on MTN and Airtel in Abuja.
  • Waterproof Test: IP68. Survived rain and hand washing. Did NOT use for swimming.

✅ Pros

  • Battery: 7 Days Battery. Charge once per week
  • Waterproof: IP68. Good for rain and gym sweat
  • Price: Under $100. Best smartwatch under ₦120,000 in Nigeria
  • Health: Accurate heart rate, sleep, and step tracking

❌ Cons

  • Screen: Gets hard to see under direct Abuja sun
  • App: Fitness app is buggy sometimes on iPhone
  • Strap: Rubber strap collects dust and sweat smell

🏆 Final Verdict: 8.0/10

Buy this if you want a cheap smartwatch for fitness, health tracking, and notifications. Best value under $100 in Nigeria 2026. Don't buy this if you need Apple Watch features, GPS accuracy for running, or premium build.
