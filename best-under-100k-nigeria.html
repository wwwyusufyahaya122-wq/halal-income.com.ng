Best Phones Under ₦100,000 in Nigeria (2026)

Updated: November 23, 2026 | By Halal Income Team | Abuja, Nigeria

If you have ₦100,000 and you want a good phone in Nigeria today, don't rush to buy. Many phones in this price look good on the poster but will frustrate you after 2 months - slow, hot, battery dies fast.

I checked prices from Jumia, Konga, Slot and phone shops in Abuja and Lagos this week. I also checked what real users are saying about battery, camera and network. These are the 5 phones I can recommend with a clear mind - no fake phones, no phones with no warranty.

Quick Answer: If you want best overall, buy Itel P55 / Tecno Spark Go 2024 type phones with 4GB RAM + 128GB storage. If you want best battery, buy a phone with 5000mAh battery. Avoid any phone with 2GB RAM in 2026 - it will hang on WhatsApp and TikTok.

1. Itel P55 - Best Overall Under 100k

Price: Around ₦95,000

This is my number 1 pick for most Nigerians. It has 4GB RAM, 128GB storage, and a 5000mAh battery that will easily last you from morning to night, even with data on. The camera is good in daylight for WhatsApp photos and small business product photos.

Pros: Fast for the price, big storage, long battery, Type-C charging.

Cons: Camera is not good at night, speaker is average.

Buy it if: You are a student, small business owner, or you want a phone that just works without stress.

2. Tecno Spark Go 2024 - Best for Students

Price: Around ₦90,000

If you are a student in Abuja, Lagos, or Kano, this phone makes sense. It is light, the battery is strong, and Tecno service centres are everywhere in Nigeria if you have a problem. It runs WhatsApp, TikTok, CapCut and Google Classroom without hanging too much.

Pros: Very easy to find parts, good battery, cheap to repair.

Cons: Only 64GB storage in some versions - buy the 128GB if you can.

3. Infinix Smart 8 - Best Camera in This Price

Price: Around ₦94,000

If you take a lot of photos for Instagram or for your business, look at Infinix Smart 8. In daylight, the photos are clear and bright. It also has a big screen, so watching YouTube is enjoyable.

Pros: Good daylight camera, big display, 128GB storage.

Cons: Will get slow if you install too many games.

4. Samsung Galaxy A05 - Best for Long Use

Price: Around ₦98,000

Samsung is more expensive for the same specs, but you are paying for trust. Samsung phones last longer, they get updates, and they don't have too many fake versions if you buy from Slot or Samsung store. If you want to use one phone for 2-3 years, this is the safest choice.

Pros: Trusted brand, clean software, good resale value.

Cons: Charger not in box, camera is average.

5. Redmi 13C - Best for TikTok and Gaming

Price: Around ₦99,000

Redmi 13C has a smooth 90Hz screen. That means scrolling on TikTok and Instagram feels faster. If you play Free Fire or small games, this one will handle it better than the others.

Pros: Smooth screen, good performance, 128GB.

Cons: Battery drains faster if you game a lot, service centres are fewer.

What to Avoid When Buying Under 100k

  • Avoid 2GB RAM phones in 2026. WhatsApp + TikTok will hang. Minimum is 4GB RAM.
  • Avoid "UK Used" phones without warranty. Many have bad batteries. If you must buy used, test battery for 1 hour first.
  • Avoid phones with no Type-C. Old Micro-USB chargers break fast and charge slowly.
  • Always collect receipt. Without receipt, no shop will repair it for free.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Which phone has the best battery under 100k?
All 5 have 5000mAh. In real life, Itel P55 and Tecno Spark Go last longest because their screens use less power.

Q: Can I get a good phone for ₦80,000?
Yes, but you will get 3GB RAM and 64GB storage. It will work for calls and WhatsApp, but not for heavy TikTok. Try to add ₦10k to reach 4GB RAM version.

Q: Is it better to buy from Jumia or shop?
Jumia is good for price check and return policy. Shop is good because you can hold the phone and test it. I recommend: Check price on Jumia, then buy from Slot or a trusted shop with receipt.

Final Verdict

Don't buy a phone because of advertisement. Buy because of RAM, storage, battery and warranty. For most Nigerians in 2026, the Itel P55 is the best balance of price and performance under ₦100,000.
