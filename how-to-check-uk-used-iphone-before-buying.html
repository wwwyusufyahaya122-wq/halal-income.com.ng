How To Check UK Used iPhone Before Buying in Nigeria (2026) - 7 Tests Before You Pay

Last Updated: November 25, 2026 | By Yusuf Yahaya, Abuja | Tested by hand in Abuja

My friend in Abuja bought a UK Used iPhone 12 for ₦320,000 in Computer Village. Seller said "Battery 95%, never opened, very clean."

After 2 weeks, Face ID stopped working. Battery dropped from 95% to 81%. When we checked Settings, it said "Unknown Part" for Display. The screen had been changed. The battery had been boosted.

He lost ₦90,000. He had to sell it for ₦230,000.

I don't want you to lose your money like that. In this guide, I will show you the 7 tests I do in the shop BEFORE I pay for any UK Used iPhone - iPhone 11, 12, 13, 14. You need only the iPhone itself and 15 minutes. No laptop needed for first 6 tests.

1. Check Battery Health - 1 Minute

Go to: Settings > Battery > Battery Health & Charging

Good: Maximum Capacity is 85% - 95% and it says "Normal" at top. No warning.

Bad: It says "Service" at top. Or Maximum Capacity is 78%, 79%. Or seller says "Battery is 100%". For UK Used iPhone 11 / 12, 100% battery in 2026 is impossible unless battery was changed. 100% = Boosted battery or new fake battery.

What I do: I check battery, then I use phone for 10 minutes - play YouTube, open camera. I check battery again. If it drops from 90% to 82% in 10 minutes, battery is boosted. Real 90% battery drops only 2% - 3% in 10 minutes.

2. Check Parts and History - 1 Minute - Most Important

Go to: Settings > General > About > Parts and Service History

On iPhone 12 and above, Apple shows you if parts were changed.

Good: It says "Genuine Apple Part" for Display and Battery. Or it says nothing - that means never opened.

Bad: It says "Unknown Part" for Display or Battery. That means screen or battery was changed with non-original part. Don't pay full price for that phone.

If seller says "I don't know about this setting", he knows. He is hiding it.

3. Check True Tone - 30 Seconds

Go to: Settings > Display & Brightness

Good: You see "True Tone" toggle. Turn it ON and OFF. Screen colour should change slightly - warmer when ON.

Bad: No True Tone option at all. That means screen was changed with fake screen. Fake screen has no True Tone chip.

This is the fastest way to catch a changed screen. I check this first before anything else.

4. Test Face ID - 1 Minute

Go to: Settings > Face ID & Passcode > Set Up Face ID

Set up your own face. It should finish in 10 seconds. Lock phone, then unlock with your face. It should unlock fast.

Bad: It says "Face ID Is Not Available" or "Unable to Activate Face ID" or it keeps failing. That means front camera / Face ID sensor was damaged when phone was opened. Repair costs ₦70,000 - ₦120,000. Don't buy.

Important: After test, delete your Face ID: Settings > Face ID > Reset Face ID. Don't leave your face in seller's phone.

5. Test Camera, Speaker, Mic, Buttons - 3 Minutes

Do this in shop, don't be shy:

  • Camera: Open camera, take photo of seller's shop, zoom 2x. Photo must be clear, not blurry. Switch to front camera, take selfie. Must be clear.
  • Speaker: Play a song on YouTube at full volume. Must be loud, no cracking sound. If speaker is low, speaker was changed.
  • Mic: Open Voice Memos, record "My name is Yusuf, testing mic" for 10 seconds, play it. Must be clear, no noise.
  • Buttons: Press Volume Up, Volume Down, Power, Mute switch 3 times each. Must click, not loose.
  • Charging Port: Plug charger. Must charge immediately. If you have to shake cable to charge, port is damaged.

6. Check iCloud, SIM Lock, and MDM - 2 Minutes

This will save you from buying a stolen phone.

  1. iCloud: Go to Settings. At top, it should say "Sign in to your iPhone" or show YOUR Apple ID if you signed in. If it shows another person's name / email and asks for password, it's iCloud locked. Return it.
  2. SIM: Put YOUR SIM inside. Make a call to someone. If it says "SIM Not Supported" or "SIM Locked", it's a locked phone from UK / US. Don't buy. Unlock costs ₦30,000+ and may fail.
  3. MDM / Supervised: Go to Settings > General > VPN & Device Management. If you see a company profile / MDM, it's a company phone. It can be locked remotely. Don't buy.
  4. Find My: Go to Settings > Your Name > Find My > Find My iPhone. It should be OFF or you can turn it OFF. If seller cannot turn it OFF, don't buy.

7. Check 3uTools Report - If Shop Has Laptop (Best Test)

If shop has laptop, ask them to connect iPhone to 3uTools (free software). It shows the truth.

Good Report: Says "Normal", Battery charge times is normal, no parts changed.

Bad Report: Says "Refurbished" in red. Or shows Battery changed, Screen changed, Front Camera changed.

If shop refuses to do 3uTools, they are hiding something. Walk to next shop that will do it. In Banex Plaza, many shops do it for ₦1,000 - ₦2,000.

✅ My 15-Minute Checklist - Copy This To Your Phone Before You Go

1. Battery 85% - 95%, no Service message
2. Parts: Genuine, no Unknown Part
3. True Tone: Present
4. Face ID: Works, sets up fast
5. Camera / Speaker / Mic: All clear
6. iCloud: No lock, SIM works, No MDM
7. 3uTools: Normal, not Refurbished

If all 7 pass = Buy it. If 1 fails = Ask discount or walk away.

⚠️ WARNING: Don't Buy These 3 Types

  • iPhone with "Boosted Battery": Battery 100% on iPhone 11 in 2026 = Fake. It will drop to 80% in 2 weeks.
  • iPhone with no receipt / no shop warranty: If shop says "No warranty, buy as you see", don't buy. Good shops give 2 weeks warranty in Abuja.
  • iPhone that is too cheap: iPhone 12 for ₦180,000 when market price is ₦320,000 = Stolen, locked, or fake. You will lose all money.

What To Do If You Already Bought Bad iPhone?

  1. Go back within 7 days with receipt. Tell seller which test failed - show him "Unknown Part" screenshot.
  2. If seller refuses, go with 2 people, not alone. Don't fight.
  3. Don't go to roadside repair to "fix Face ID". They will spoil it more. Use Apple authorised repair or trusted shop.
  4. Next time, use my 7 tests before you pay.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is UK Used iPhone better than new?

UK Used is cheaper, but you must check it. New iPhone 12 is almost not available in 2026. UK Used is okay if all 7 tests pass and shop gives warranty.

What is battery boost?

Battery boost is when seller uses software to make 78% battery show as 95%. It drops fast after 2 weeks. You can catch it by using phone for 10 minutes in shop - boosted battery drops fast.

Should I buy iPhone with Unknown Part?

Only if you know and price is very low. Unknown Part screen means touch may fail later, True Tone will not work, and resale value will be low. I don't recommend for first iPhone.

How long should UK Used iPhone battery last?

Good 88% battery iPhone 12 should last 6-7 hours of normal use - WhatsApp, YouTube, calls. If it dies in 3 hours, battery is bad.
