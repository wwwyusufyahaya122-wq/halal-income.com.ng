About Us

Halal Income Tech was founded in 2024 by a team of tech enthusiasts based in Abuja, Nigeria. We got tired of buying fake gadgets and overpriced products online from Jumia, Konga, and social media vendors.

So we decided to do something different. We spend our own money to buy, test, and review gadgets the way Nigerians actually use them.

Our Mission

To help Nigerians buy original, affordable, and durable gadgets by testing them first in Nigerian conditions. No fake promises. No sponsored lies.

How We Test Products

  1. We Buy It: We don't accept free products. We pay from Jumia, Slot, Konga, Amazon so reviews are 100% honest.
  2. We Test It: 14 days minimum. We test battery during NEPA, performance in 35°C heat, and call quality in Lagos traffic.
  3. We Check Nigeria Factors: Is voltage 220V? Can it ship to Port Harcourt? Is warranty valid at Slot or 3Star?
  4. We Update Prices: Prices in ₦aira updated weekly because dollar and import duties change.

No Sponsored Reviews

If a product is bad, we will tell you. If it's good, we will tell you. Brands cannot pay us to lie. Our income comes from affiliate links and ads, not from brands.
