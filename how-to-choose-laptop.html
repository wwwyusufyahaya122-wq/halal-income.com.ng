7 Things Nigerians Must Check Before Buying a Laptop

Buying a laptop in Nigeria is different. Here is what matters:

  1. Battery Life: Must be 8hrs+ for NEPA
  2. 220V Charger: Avoid US 110V only laptops
  3. Heat Resistance: Can it survive 35°C?
  4. Warranty in Nigeria: Is there service center in Lagos/Abuja?
  5. Weight: For commuting in traffic
  6. SSD Storage: Faster and less power
  7. Price in Naira: Including duty
