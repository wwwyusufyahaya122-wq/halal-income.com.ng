How To Choose A Laptop For University in Nigeria (Without Wasting ₦500k)

By: Yusuf Yahaya - Abuja | Updated: November 20, 2026 | Read time: 9 min

Don't buy laptop by "Core i7" name. Buy by battery, RAM, and weight. I explain what a student in UNILAG, ABU, or UNIJOS actually needs - and what to avoid in Computer Village.

Quick Rule: If laptop has 8GB RAM + SSD + 8 hours battery + under 2kg, it is good for 90% of students. Don't pay extra for "i7" if RAM is 4GB.

1. RAM: 8GB Minimum, Not 4GB

Chrome + Zoom + Word + WhatsApp Web = uses 6GB already. If you buy 4GB, laptop will freeze during online class. 8GB is minimum. 16GB only if you study engineering, programming, video editing, or architecture.

2. Storage: SSD, Not HDD

SSD laptop boots in 15 seconds. HDD laptop boots in 2 minutes and breaks if you drop your bag in hostel. Always ask: "Is this SSD?" Check in shop: Settings > Storage - it should say SSD. If seller says "HDD is okay", walk away.

3. Battery: Test It In Shop For 1 Hour

Don't trust seller "Battery is 10 hours". Test it. Ask seller to unplug charger, use laptop for 1 hour in shop. If battery drops from 100% to 70% in 1 hour, real battery is about 3 hours - too low. Good battery drops 100% to 88-90% in 1 hour.

For NEPA: You need 8 hours battery. Lectures + hostel + NEPA = you can't charge always. Carry 20000mAh power bank with laptop charging support if your laptop charges with Type-C.

4. UK Used Laptop - How To Avoid Scam

Mistake to avoid: 70% of UK Used laptops in Nigeria have bad battery. Don't buy UK Used with no warranty.

If you must buy UK Used:

1. Test battery for 1 hour in shop.
2. Test ALL keys - type A to Z, test spacebar, test touchpad.
3. Check charger is original, says 100-240V.
4. Ask for 30-day warranty IN WRITING. If seller says "No warranty", don't buy.

5. What To Buy By Course

  • Arts, Law, Education, Social Science: 8GB RAM, 256GB SSD, Core i3 / Ryzen 3 is enough. Don't waste money on i7.
  • Engineering, Computer Science, Architecture: 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, Core i5 / Ryzen 5, dedicated graphics if you do AutoCAD.
  • Video Editing / Content Creation: 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, good screen, battery 8 hours.

Questions Students Ask Me

Q: Is Core i7 better than Core i5?
A: Not always. Core i5 with 8GB RAM + SSD is faster than Core i7 with 4GB RAM + HDD. Check RAM and SSD first, processor second.
Q: Should I buy HP, Lenovo, or Dell?
A: For students, all 3 are good if specs are same. Buy the one with better battery + better warranty in Nigeria, not by brand name.
Q: How much should I budget?
A: Don't buy cheapest. A good student laptop with 8GB + SSD + 8hr battery will last 4 years of university. Cheap laptop will break in 1 year and you will buy again.
