Buying Guide: 5 Things Nigerians Must Check Before Buying Earbuds

  1. Battery Life in Heat: Nigeria is hot. Cheap batteries die fast. Look for 30hrs+ total.
  2. Call Quality: Test with noise cancellation. Lagos traffic is loud. You need 2+ mics.
  3. Water Resistance: IPX4 minimum for sweat and rain. IPX5 better for gym.
  4. Warranty in Nigeria: Buy brands with service centers: Oraimo, Soundcore, Havit.
  5. Avoid Fakes: If price is too low on WhatsApp, it's fake. Buy from Jumia Mall or Slot.
