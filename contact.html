Welcome to Halal Income Nigeria

Assalamu Alaikum! I'm the founder of Halal Income Nigeria, and I created this website in 2021 for one simple reason: buying tech in Nigeria is stressful, and finding halal ways to earn online is even harder.

Our Mission

To help every Nigerian make smart tech buying decisions and discover legitimate, halal income opportunities online - without scams, without riba, and without fake promises.

Why We Started This Site

For 5 years, I was buying gadgets from Amazon and shipping them to Abuja, Lagos, and Port Harcourt. 3 out of 5 times, I got fake products, wrong chargers, or things that couldn't survive Nigerian heat and NEPA. I got tired of wasting money.

So I started testing everything myself and sharing honest reviews. No brand pays us. If we don't like it, we say it.

Our 4-Step Testing Process

  1. We Buy It With Our Own Money: No brand pays us. No free products. Full honesty.
  2. We Test It For 14 Days: Battery, speed, durability, Nigerian heat + 220V + NEPA surges
  3. We Check Halal Income Potential: Can this gadget help you earn halal income online?
  4. We Verify Nigeria Shipping: Warranty + Naira price + duty + 220V charger
