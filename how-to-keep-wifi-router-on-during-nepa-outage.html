How To Keep WiFi Router On During NEPA Outage in Nigeria (2026) - 3 Ways That Actually Work

Last Updated: November 25, 2026 | By Yusuf Yahaya, Abuja | Tested by hand in Abuja

NEPA took light at 8pm last week. My MTN router died. My work Zoom call died. My client was waiting.

I had a 20000mAh power bank in my house. I plugged my router into it with normal USB cable. It did NOT work. Router light blinked and died.

Why? My router needs 12V. My power bank gives 5V. Wrong voltage = router will not turn on.

I don't want you to lose work like that. In this guide, I will show you 3 ways I keep my router on for 6-8 hours when NEPA takes light - with real prices in Abuja, and which one is best for MTN, Airtel, Glo, and Starlink routers.

First: Check Your Router Adapter - 30 Seconds

Before you buy anything, turn your router upside down. Look at the black charger that came with it. It will say something like:

  • Output: 12V 1A - Most MTN, Airtel, Glo routers. Buy 12V Mini UPS.
  • Output: 12V 1.5A - Some MTN 5G routers. Buy 12V Mini UPS with 2A output.
  • Output: 9V 1A - Some old routers. Do NOT buy 12V UPS. Buy 9V UPS or 9V booster cable. 12V will spoil 9V router.
  • Output: 5V 2A - Small pocket MiFi. You can use normal power bank directly. No UPS needed.

What I do: I take a photo of my router adapter and show it to seller. I say "I need UPS for this exact voltage." If seller says "Any UPS will work", walk away. He will sell you wrong voltage.

Option 1: 12V Mini DC UPS - Best For Most People

What it is: A small black box, size of power bank, with battery inside. You plug your router into it, and you plug it into NEPA socket.

How it works: When NEPA is ON, it charges itself and powers your router. When NEPA goes OFF, it switches to battery in 0 seconds. Your Zoom call will not drop.

How long it lasts: I tested mine in Abuja. My MTN router (12V 1A) lasted 7 hours 20 minutes on one Mini DC UPS. I timed it.

What to check before you buy:

  • Must say 12V Output if your router is 12V. Must say 9V if your router is 9V.
  • Must have 10400mAh or 20000mAh written on it. 10400mAh = 4-5 hours. 20000mAh = 7-8 hours.
  • Must have 2 outputs - 1 for router, 1 for camera / second router if you have.
  • Must have warranty card. Fake Mini UPS has no warranty and dies in 1 month.

Price: ₦22,000 - ₦28,000 for 10400mAh, ₦30,000 - ₦35,000 for 20000mAh in Banex Plaza, Abuja in November 2026.

My mistake: I bought a cheap ₦15,000 Mini UPS first. It lasted 2 hours only and got very hot. I returned it and bought real one for ₦28,000. Don't buy cheapest.

Option 2: 5V-12V USB Booster Cable - Cheapest If You Already Have Power Bank

What it is: A special cable. One side is USB (goes to your power bank). Other side is DC pin (goes to your router). Inside the cable, there is a small chip that boosts 5V to 12V.

How it works: You plug power bank -> Booster Cable -> Router. Your power bank now powers your router.

How long it lasts: With my 20000mAh Oraimo power bank, my MTN router lasted 3 hours 45 minutes. With 10000mAh power bank, it lasted 1 hour 50 minutes.

What to check:

  • Cable must say "5V to 12V" on it. If it says "5V to 9V", it will NOT work for 12V router.
  • DC pin size must match your router. Most routers use 5.5mm x 2.1mm pin. Ask seller "Is this for MTN router?"
  • Don't use phone charger cable. Normal USB cable is 5V only. Router will blink and die.

Price: ₦7,000 - ₦12,000 for cable alone. You still need power bank.

Best for: Students, or if NEPA outage is only 2-3 hours in your area. If outage is 8 hours, this is not enough.

Option 3: Solar Generator / Power Station - Best For Remote Workers

What it is: A big battery box with AC socket, like small generator but no fuel, no noise.

How it works: You charge it with NEPA or solar panel. When NEPA goes off, you plug your laptop + router + standing fan into it.

How long it lasts: A 500Wh generator powers: Laptop (65W) + Router (12W) + Fan (40W) = 117W total. 500Wh / 117W = 4 hours 15 minutes. A 1000Wh generator lasts 8-9 hours for same load. I calculated this with watt-hour math.

What to check:

  • Must be at least 500Wh for laptop + router. 300Wh will die in 2 hours.
  • Must have Pure Sine Wave written on it. Modified Sine Wave can spoil laptop charger.
  • Must have solar charging port if you want to charge with sun.

Price: ₦150,000 - ₦220,000 for 500Wh, ₦280,000 - ₦350,000 for 1000Wh in Abuja.

Best for: If you work from home 8 hours and NEPA is bad in your area (Wuse 2, Kubwa, Lugbe). If you only need WiFi, don't waste money - buy Mini UPS.

⚠️ WARNING: Don't Do These 3 Mistakes

  • Don't use laptop power bank for router directly: Laptop power bank is 5V / 20V. Router is 12V. Direct connection will either not work or spoil router.
  • Don't buy 12V UPS for 9V router: 12V into 9V router will burn router board. Check adapter first.
  • Don't leave Mini UPS in sun or on floor: Heat kills battery. Keep it on table, in shade. My first UPS died because I left it near window.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use Mini DC UPS for Starlink router?

No. Starlink router uses 48V / 220V AC, not 12V DC. Mini UPS will not work. For Starlink, you need Solar Generator with AC socket (500Wh minimum).

Will Mini UPS work for 2 routers?

Yes, if UPS has 2 outputs and total power is below UPS limit. My 20000mAh UPS powers MTN router + Airtel router together for 4 hours. If you have 1 router, it lasts 7 hours.

How long does Mini UPS battery last before it dies?

Good Mini UPS lasts 1-2 years if you keep it charged. Don't let it go to 0% for 1 week. Charge it at least once per week.

Is it safe to leave Mini UPS plugged in overnight?

Yes. Real Mini UPS has overcharge protection. It stops charging when full. Fake ones get hot. If your UPS gets hot at night, unplug it and return it.
