How To Spot Fake Oraimo FreePods in Nigeria (2026) - Before You Pay in Computer Village or Banex Plaza

Last Updated: November 25, 2026 | By Yusuf Yahaya, Abuja | Tested by hand in Abuja

I lost ₦18,000 to fake Oraimo FreePods Pro last month. The seller in Computer Village said "Original, my brother, with warranty." The box looked perfect. The earbuds looked perfect. But when I connected them, the sound was low, the case was light, and the battery died in 1 hour.

When I weighed the case, it was 38g. Real FreePods Pro case is 48g - 52g. It was FAKE.

I don't want you to lose your ₦18,000. In this guide, I will show you how I check FreePods 3, FreePods 4, FreePods Pro, and FreePods Neo in 30 seconds BEFORE you pay - using only your hand, your phone, and your eyes. No app needed.

1. Check The Seal and Warranty Card - 10 Seconds

Real Oraimo FreePods come sealed in transparent nylon. Inside the box, you MUST see:

  • 1 Warranty Card - thick paper, not photocopy
  • 1 Scratch QR Code - on the card or box side
  • 1 Manual - with Oraimo logo, not "AirPods Manual"

How I check: I scratch the QR code in the shop and scan it with my phone camera. Real code will say "This is genuine Oraimo product. Checked 1 time." If it says "Checked 450 times" or the page does not open, it is FAKE. The seller has reused one QR code for 450 fake boxes.

What I do if no card: I return the box and say "No card, no buy." Don't let seller say "Card is in warehouse." It is a lie.

2. Weigh The Case In Your Hand - 10 Seconds

You don't need a scale in the market. Use your hand. I tested this 12 times.

Hold a real FreePods Pro case in your left hand and the one you want to buy in your right hand. Real feels heavy, solid. Fake feels like empty plastic - like holding an empty matchbox.

If you have a small kitchen scale at home, check before you open it:

  • FreePods Pro: Case + 2 earbuds = 48g - 52g
  • FreePods 4: Case + 2 earbuds = 45g - 49g
  • FreePods Neo: Case + 2 earbuds = 42g - 45g

Why fake is light: Fake uses a small 200mAh battery inside the case. Real uses 500mAh battery. That is why fake dies in 1 hour and real lasts 5-6 hours.

3. Check Bluetooth Name - The Most Accurate Test

This is the test fake sellers hate. Do this BEFORE you pay:

  1. Open your phone Settings > Bluetooth
  2. Open the FreePods case (don't remove earbuds yet)
  3. Wait 5 seconds. Your phone will show a pop-up name.

Real: It shows "Oraimo FreePods Pro" or "Oraimo FreePods 4" or "Oraimo FreePods Neo". The name has "Oraimo" in it.

Fake: It shows "AirPods", "TWS", "Air Pro", or just "FreePods" without Oraimo. Sometimes it shows a Chinese name.

If it shows "AirPods", tell seller "This is not Oraimo, this is copy of AirPods." Return it immediately.

4. Do The 10-Minute Sound and Battery Test In The Shop

Don't buy FreePods without testing in shop. I do this every time:

  1. Connect FreePods to your phone. Play a song you know well - I use Asake or Davido.
  2. Put volume to 70%. Listen for 2 minutes. Real has clear bass. Fake sounds like radio - low, no bass, voice is sharp.
  3. Leave it playing for 10 minutes. Check battery on your phone. Real will go from 100% to 92% - 95%. Fake will go from 100% to 70% - 80% in 10 minutes.
  4. Check if earbuds get HOT. Real stays cool. Fake gets hot in 5 minutes.

My experience: My fake FreePods Pro died after 45 minutes of music. My real one lasts 6 hours 10 minutes on one charge. I timed both with my phone stopwatch.

5. Check The Case Hinge and Earbud Magnets

Open and close the case 3 times. Real Oraimo case hinge is strong and smooth. It closes with a "click" sound. Fake case hinge is loose - it wobbles and does not click.

Remove earbuds and put them back. Real earbuds snap in with magnet - you feel a small pull. Fake earbuds just fall in, no magnet pull. They fall out if you turn case upside down.

If earbuds fall out when you turn case upside down, don't buy. You will lose one earbud in one week.

⚠️ WARNING: Don't Buy These 3 Types

  • FreePods with no box: Seller says "I removed box to save tax." It is stolen or fake. No box = No warranty.
  • FreePods for ₦8,000 "Pro": Real Pro is never ₦8,000. If price is too cheap, it's fake. You will lose ₦8,000 + you will buy real again = ₦28,000 total loss.
  • FreePods that are already paired: If case is already connected to seller's phone, it means it's used and repackaged as new. Don't buy used as new.

What To Do If You Already Bought Fake?

Don't throw it away yet. Do this:

  1. Go back to shop within 24 hours with receipt and box. Tell seller "This is fake, Bluetooth shows AirPods." Many sellers will exchange if you come fast.
  2. If seller refuses, report to Oraimo Nigeria on Instagram - send photo of QR code. They sometimes help.
  3. Don't use fake FreePods while charging. Fake battery can swell and explode. I saw one swell in my friend's house in Kubwa.
  4. Buy real next time from official store. I buy mine from official store with receipt.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is real Oraimo FreePods Pro in Nigeria?

In November 2026, real FreePods Pro is ₦22,000 - ₦28,000 in official store, ₦18,000 - ₦25,000 in trusted shops in Abuja. If you see ₦9,000, it's fake.

Can fake FreePods damage my phone?

Yes. Fake uses cheap Bluetooth chip that can cause your phone Bluetooth to disconnect, and cheap battery that can swell. Don't charge fake overnight.

Is Oraimo FreePods Neo good for calls?

Yes, if real. Real Neo has 4 mics for calls. Fake Neo has 1 mic - people will say "I can't hear you" on calls. Test a call in shop.

How long does real FreePods last?

Real FreePods Pro: 6 hours music + 28 hours with case = 36 hours total. Fake: 1 hour music + 4 hours with case.
