This Cookie Policy explains what cookies are, what cookies we use on Halal Income Tech, and how you can control them. By using our website, you agree to our use of cookies as described below. We use CookieYes to ask for your consent before we set non-essential cookies.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your phone, laptop, or tablet when you visit a website. They help the site remember you, remember your preferences, and show you relevant ads. Cookies do not give us access to your phone or personal files.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Cookies

We use Google AdSense to show ads on this site. Google and its partners use cookies to:

  • Show ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites
  • Measure how many people see and click ads
  • Prevent fraud and show safe ads

You can learn how Google uses your data here: Google Advertising Policies and Google Privacy Policy.

You can turn off personalised ads by Google here: Google Ads Settings.

4. How To Control Cookies

You have full control. You can:

  1. Use Our Banner: When you first visit, click "Reject" or "Customise" on the CookieYes banner to block advertising cookies.
  2. Browser Settings: In Chrome, go to Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies > Block third-party cookies.
  3. Delete Cookies: In Chrome, go to Settings > Privacy > Clear browsing data > Cookies.

If you block cookies, our site will still work, but some features like remembering your preference may not work, and you may see less relevant ads.

5. Do We Sell Your Data?

No. We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your name, phone number, or WhatsApp number with advertisers. Google AdSense uses anonymous cookie data only to show ads.

We may update this Cookie Policy if Google changes its rules or if we add new tools. We will update the "Last Updated" date at the top. Please check this page from time to time.
