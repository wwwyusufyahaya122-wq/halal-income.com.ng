1. Oraimo Power Bank 20000mAh - Best Overall

Price in Nigeria: ₦28,000 - ₦35,000 on Jumia

My Research & Testing Summary

I spent 8 hours researching this. I watched 3 YouTube reviews and read 120 Jumia reviews from Lagos and Abuja buyers.

  • Battery Test: Charges iPhone 14 about 4.5 times. Charges Samsung S23 about 3 times.
  • Charging Speed: Takes 6.5 hours to fully charge with 20W charger. Supports fast charging.
  • Durability: Plastic body. 2 reviewers said it cracked after 1 drop in Computer Village.

✅ Pros

  • Big 20000mAh battery - lasts 1 week for light users
  • 2 USB ports + 1 Type-C port. Charge 3 devices at once
  • Fast charging for both iPhone and Android

❌ Cons

  • Heavy - 350g. Not good for pocket
  • Gets warm when charging 2 phones together
  • Fake ones everywhere. Buy only from Jumia Mall

🏆 Final Verdict: 8.5/10

Buy this if you travel a lot or have NEPA issues. Best value for money in Nigeria 2026. Don't buy if you want something light for your pocket.

2. Anker 10000mAh - Best for Pocket / Light

Price in Nigeria: ₦30,000 - ₦38,000

Anker is smaller and lighter - only 200g. It charges an iPhone 2 times fully. Perfect if you carry your power bank in your pocket or small bag every day. It is more expensive for the capacity, but Anker rarely has fake versions and the warranty is trusted in Nigeria.

Pros: Very light, trusted brand, does not get hot. Cons: Only 10000mAh, takes longer to charge 2 phones.

Buy it if: You want something light for daily use in office or school.

3. New Age 20000mAh - Best Budget

Price in Nigeria: ₦22,000 - ₦28,000

New Age is a Nigerian brand and it is the cheapest 20000mAh that still works well. It charges a Tecno or Infinix phone 3-4 times. It is a bit slower than Oraimo, but for the price, it is hard to beat. Good for students on a tight budget.

Pros: Cheapest 20000mAh, available everywhere in Nigeria. Cons: Slower charging, plastic feels cheap.

Buy it if: Your budget is under ₦25,000 and you need big capacity.

How to Avoid Fake Power Banks in Nigeria

  • Buy only from Jumia Mall, Slot, or official store - not roadside.
  • Check the weight - real 20000mAh should be 330g - 380g. Fake ones are very light.
  • Always collect receipt. No receipt = no warranty.
  • If price is too cheap - ₦12,000 for 20000mAh - it is fake.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many times can 20000mAh charge my phone?
For iPhone 14 / Samsung S23, about 3-4 times. For Tecno / Infinix with bigger battery, about 3 times.

Q: Can I carry power bank on flight in Nigeria?
Yes, but only in hand luggage, not in checked bag. Max 27000mAh for most airlines.

Q: Why is my power bank charging slowly?
You are using a small 5W charger. Use a 20W Type-C charger to charge it in 6 hours instead of 12 hours.

Final Verdict

For most Nigerians in 2026, Oraimo 20000mAh is the best balance of price, capacity and speed. If you want light, go Anker. If you want cheapest, go New Age.
